Sometimes the problem isn’t discipline, consistency, or motivation.

It’s that you’re tired. Overstimulated. Disconnected from your work.

And trying to “push through” usually makes it worse.

This Creator 3-Day Reset Plan is a soft reset — not a productivity challenge. It’s designed to help you reconnect with your work in a way that feels manageable again.

No strict schedule. No pressure to perform. Just three small shifts to help you start again.

**How it works:**

Each day has a different focus:

- **Day 1 — Reset:** step back, reduce noise, and lower the pressure
- **Day 2 — Reconnect:** gently re-engage with your ideas and content
- **Day 3 — Restart:** create something small and doable

You don’t need to complete everything perfectly. The goal is momentum, not output.

**What’s inside:**

- A simple 3-day structure that doesn’t overwhelm
- Low, medium, and optional tasks for each day
- Prompts to help you reconnect with your ideas
- Space to reflect without overthinking
- A flexible approach that works with fluctuating energy

**Who it’s for:**

This reset is for creators who:

- Feel stuck or avoidant around their work
- Are dealing with burnout, fatigue, or overwhelm
- Haven’t created in a while and don’t know where to start
- Need a softer re-entry into their workflow

It’s especially helpful if traditional “get back on track” advice feels too intense or unrealistic.

**How to use it:**

Download the reset plan from Ko-fi and go through it over three days — or at your own pace.

You can stretch it out if needed. Repeat days. Skip things that don’t help.

This isn’t about fixing yourself. It’s about making creating feel accessible again.
