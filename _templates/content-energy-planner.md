Most content systems assume you can create at full capacity every day — consistently, predictably, without friction.

That’s not how energy works. And it’s definitely not how neurodivergent creators work.

This Content Energy Planner is built around a simple shift: instead of forcing yourself to match your plan, your plan adapts to your energy.

On low-energy days, you maintain. On medium days, you refine. On high-energy days, you create.

No guilt. No falling behind. Just a clear place to start, every day.

**What’s inside:**

- A simple energy-based system — low (maintain), medium (refine), high (create)
- A weekly overview to set direction without overplanning
- Daily pages with energy check-ins and realistic task sections
- “Must do” and “nice to do” lists to reduce pressure
- Optional gentle time blocks (only if they help)
- Brain dump spaces to clear mental clutter
- End-of-day reflection prompts — short and low-effort
- Built-in reminders for basic needs (water, food, rest, movement)

**Who it’s for:**

This planner is for creators who don’t have consistent energy — whether that’s due to ADHD, chronic illness, burnout, or just being human.

It works especially well if you:

- Struggle with rigid content schedules
- Feel guilty on low-energy days
- Start the week with big plans that don’t match reality
- Need a softer, more sustainable workflow

**How to use it:**

Download the planner from Ko-fi and use it digitally or print it.

Start each day with an honest energy check-in — not what you _want_ your energy to be, but what it actually is. Then match your tasks to that level.

Use the weekly overview to guide your direction, not control it. And when your brain gets crowded, use the brain dump instead of trying to hold everything at once.

That’s it. No complicated system to maintain.
