Most analytics tools give you data.

Numbers. Tables. Dashboards you have to interpret yourself.

But when you're already low on energy, even opening those dashboards can feel like too much.

Automated Creator Reports is a small desktop app that takes your analytics and turns them into something actually usable — clear, structured reports you can read in minutes.

No dashboards to navigate. No manual sorting. No extra steps.

Just drop in your file, and the report is created for you.

**Current status:**

This tool is currently in development.

The core idea is working, and the first version focuses on **Ko-fi analytics** — with support for other platforms planned later.

If you're interested, you can follow along or support early development below.

**What it will do:**

- Convert CSV analytics into clean Markdown reports
- Automatically generate monthly and quarterly summaries
- Highlight top-performing products or content first
- Include full breakdowns without clutter
- Work locally on your device (no uploads, no tracking)

**Why this exists:**

Most tools are built for depth.

This is built for clarity.

It’s designed for creators who:

- Feel overwhelmed by analytics dashboards
- Want quick insights without digging through data
- Prefer simple, readable summaries over complex tools
- Need something that works even on low-energy days

**How it will work:**

When it’s ready, you’ll be able to:

1. Export your analytics as a CSV file
2. Drag and drop it into the app
3. Instantly get a structured report saved to your folder

No setup. No accounts. No learning curve.
